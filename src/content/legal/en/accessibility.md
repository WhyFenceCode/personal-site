order: 4
---
This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

- The system cursor is never hidden.
- Animation, the ink effect and the 3D name follow your system's reduced-motion setting, and can be switched off
  with the "Motion" button in the name block or the footer. The scrolling band can be paused.
- The 3D name is decorative; the name is always available as text.
- Every page works with a keyboard alone, with visible focus.
- Links to other websites open in a new tab and say so to screen readers.

If something doesn't work for you, write to **{{email}}** and you'll get a reply within five working days.
