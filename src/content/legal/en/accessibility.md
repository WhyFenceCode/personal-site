This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

- With a mouse, the pointer is shown as a dot that inverts what is under it. The system cursor comes back over
  embedded websites and whenever your system asks for high or increased contrast. Touch and keyboard are unaffected.
- Animation, the ink effect and the 3D name follow your system's reduced-motion setting, and can be switched off
  with the "Motion" button in the name block or the footer. The scrolling band can be paused.
- The intro that plays when you open the site (the ink, and the typed welcome line) is skipped entirely with reduced
  motion or motion off, has a Skip button, and is hidden from screen readers, which hear "Loading" instead.
- The 3D name is decorative; the name is always available as text.
- Every page works with a keyboard alone, with visible focus.
- Blog posts and the documentation use real headings in order, each with its own link, and a contents list to jump
  between sections. Images in posts are written with text alternatives.
- Embedded websites never load by themselves: a Continue button, reachable by keyboard, says what loading one means
  first. Embedded sites are other people's pages and may not meet this standard.
- Code viewers list their files as buttons in a labelled list; the open file's name is announced when it changes,
  and every file also has a link to the same file on GitHub.
- Links to other websites open in a new tab and say so to screen readers.
