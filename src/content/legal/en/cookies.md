title: Cookies and storage
order: 2
---
This site sets no cookies. A few preferences are kept in your browser's local storage:

| Name | What it remembers | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `site.lang` | Your language | Until you clear it |
| `site.motion` | Whether you turned motion off | Until you clear it |
| `site.banner` | That you dismissed the top banner | Until you clear it |
| `site.consent` | Your statistics choice (only if consent is asked for) | Until you clear it |
| `site.seen` | That you've seen the intro (session storage) | Until you close the tab |

None of these are sent anywhere.
