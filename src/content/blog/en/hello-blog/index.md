## Why a blog

The projects page shows what I finished. It doesn't show how I got there: the dead ends, the fixes, the things I learned on the way. This is where that goes.

## What to expect

Short posts, written when there is something worth writing down. Mostly:

- Engineering projects and what went wrong in them
- Notes on the tools I use to build this site
- Sailing, now and then

The newest post is always at [/blog/newest](/en/blog/newest/), so that link stays current.
