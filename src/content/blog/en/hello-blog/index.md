## Why a blog

Sometimes I have some cool ideas about what I am currently working on, or design things that I would like to share, so I decided to add a blog page to do so. From cool code projects that I have recently finished, recent things that I did at school, and how to tutorials, they will all be here.

## What to expect

I will post here when I feel like it and find the time to, this could be every week, or every month, or even less. I plan to write about what im doing or have done, and it won't be too frequent.
